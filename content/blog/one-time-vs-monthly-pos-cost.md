A monthly fee is easy to say yes to. PKR 2,000 is less than a day's takings for most shops, and it does not feel like a decision.

That is exactly why it is worth doing the arithmetic once.

## The numbers

Comparing a **PKR 2,000/month** subscription against a **PKR 30,000 one-time** licence:

| After | Rented | Owned | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 months | PKR 12,000 | PKR 30,000 | Rent is ahead |
| 1 year | PKR 24,000 | PKR 30,000 | Rent is ahead |
| **15 months** | **PKR 30,000** | **PKR 30,000** | **Crossover** |
| 2 years | PKR 48,000 | PKR 30,000 | Owning saves 18,000 |
| 3 years | PKR 72,000 | PKR 30,000 | Owning saves 42,000 |
| 5 years | PKR 120,000 | PKR 30,000 | Owning saves 90,000 |

Rent genuinely is cheaper at first. That is not marketing — it is true for the first fifteen months. After that the gap only widens, because one side stops paying and the other does not.

Do this with your own numbers. If you are quoted PKR 1,500 a month, the crossover moves to twenty months. If you are looking at a PKR 55,000 package, it moves further out. The shape does not change.

## The part the table does not show

**When you stop paying, you stop having software.** That is the real difference between renting and owning, and it does not appear in any monthly figure.

With a subscription, your sales history, your stock list and your customer balances live behind a payment. Miss it and access goes — usually not the data itself, but your ability to reach it, which amounts to the same thing on a Tuesday morning with customers waiting.

With a licence, a bad month is a bad month. The till still opens.

## When renting is the right choice

Be honest about these, because sometimes it is:

- **You are testing whether software suits you at all.** A few months of rent is a cheaper experiment than a licence.
- **The business itself is uncertain.** A shop that may not exist in a year should not buy a five-year asset.
- **You genuinely need live multi-branch access** and are willing to pay for the infrastructure behind it.
- **You want somebody else responsible for backups** and will not maintain your own.

## When owning is the right choice

- The shop has been running for years and will keep running
- You want the same predictable cost every year, which is zero
- You would rather your data sat on your own machine
- Losing access for non-payment is not an acceptable failure mode
