Ask three POS vendors in Pakistan for a price and you will get three answers that cannot be compared, because they are not selling the same thing. One quotes a monthly fee. One quotes a licence. One quotes "starting from" a number that does not include installation.

Here is how to read those quotes properly.

## The two pricing models

**Monthly subscription.** You pay every month for as long as you use the software, commonly **PKR 1,500 to 2,000**. Stop paying and you lose access — usually including the reports and history you built up.

**One-time licence.** You pay once, commonly **PKR 30,000 to 80,000**, and the software is yours. Support and updates are often a separate optional yearly fee afterwards.

Neither is automatically better. It depends entirely on how long you intend to use it.

## What each costs over time

At PKR 2,000 a month against a PKR 30,000 one-time licence:

| After | Rented | Bought once |
|---|---|---|
| 1 year | PKR 24,000 | PKR 30,000 |
| 2 years | PKR 48,000 | PKR 30,000 |
| 3 years | PKR 72,000 | PKR 30,000 |
| 5 years | PKR 120,000 | PKR 30,000 |

Rent genuinely wins in year one. The crossover is around **15 months**. After that the gap widens every month and never closes.

So the honest question is not "which is cheaper" — it is **how long will this shop be running?** If the answer is more than about eighteen months, buying costs less.

## What quotes leave out

Ask about these explicitly, because they are where a low headline price gets made up:

- **Installation and setup** — is it included, or billed separately?
- **Entering your products** — someone has to type in your catalogue. Who, and at what cost?
- **Staff training** — included, or per session?
- **Support after the first year** — what does it cost, and what happens if you do not renew?
- **A second counter or branch** — a new licence, or covered?
- **Your data if you leave** — can you export it, and in what format?

That last one matters more than people expect. Software you cannot leave is software with a price that can rise.

## Hardware is separate

Software price is not system price. Budget separately for a **thermal receipt printer** and a **barcode scanner** if you want fast billing. Most shops already have a workable Windows PC or laptop, so that is often not a new cost.
